Adjoiners in seconds: step by step

This is exactly what you do in Carlson, IntelliCAD and AutoCAD to get every adjoiner's owner, deed and plat label, the adjoiner table and the letters. Real screens, no editing tricks.

  1. Find your parcel

    Type BCS. At the prompt, choose PIN (or Address, or pick a point in the drawing). BIGCS pulls the parcel from the county GIS and draws it in the right place.

  2. The owner label is already there

    The parcel comes in labeled: owner, deed book and page, plat reference and PIN. Nothing typed.

  3. Get all the adjoiners at once

    Run BCS again and pick Adjoiners at the Mode prompt, then click inside the subject parcel. Every neighbor comes in with the same label.

  4. Every neighbor, done

    Here is the whole job: the subject and all its adjoiners, each labeled from county GIS.

  5. Drop in the adjoiner table

    Type BCSADJ, pick the subject parcel, then click where the table's top-left corner goes. You get owner, mailing address and site address for every adjoiner, plus a CSV saved next to your drawing.

  6. Make the adjoiner letters

    Type BCSLTR. Pick the parcels, pick a template (letters or door hangers) and click Create.

  7. Letters ready to mail

    Letters come out as Word documents with your company info, plus mailing labels.

The commands
BCSParcels with labels. Locate by Pick, LatLon, StatePlane, Address or PIN. Mode: Single, Adjoiners, Radius or Window.
BCSTXTLabels only, no parcel boundary.
BCSADJAdjoiner table plus a CSV.
BCSLTRAdjoiner letters and door hangers.
BCSSTLabel style: how deeds and plats read.
BCSREFRefresh labels already in the drawing.

BIGCS is not a survey

Labels come as-is from county GIS records. Always confirm against the recorded deeds and plats. Read the disclaimer.
