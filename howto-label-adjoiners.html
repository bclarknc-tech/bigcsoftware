How to Label Adjoiners in Carlson

Every boundary job needs the adjoiners on the drawing: who owns the land next door, and where their deed and plat are recorded. Here is how to pull that from county GIS and label it in Carlson, IntelliCAD or full AutoCAD with BIGCS, instead of retyping it from the county site.

1. Set your label style once

Run BCSST to open the label style editor. Set the line order, wording, SHX or TrueType fonts, text height, justification and layers, with a live preview. Styles are saved and reused, so your adjoiner labels come out in your own CAD standards on every job.

2. Find the subject parcel

Run BCS (G2C and GIS2CAD also work). Locate the parcel by Pick, Lat/Lon, State Plane N/E, Address or PIN. If you have the PIN from the deed or the tax card, that is the quickest way to land on the right parcel.

3. Pick the adjoiners

Choose the Adjoiners mode in BCS. BIGCS draws the parcel boundaries at true coordinates, straight from county GIS. Data comes as-is from each county's public GIS. BIGCS never adjusts or datum-shifts it.

4. Check the labels

Each adjoiner gets a label with the owner, deed book/page, plat book/page and PIN, in the style you set. A label looks like this:

N/F
JOHN D. SMITH
DB 14256 PG 198
MB 42 PG 12
PIN: 045-122-09
555 SAMPLE WAY
CHARLOTTE, NC 28202

Sample label with fictional data. Line order, fonts and justification are configurable.

Need only the text, without the parcel linework? Use BCSTXT. Each object also records which GIS source it came from and when it was pulled.

Labels come straight from each county's public GIS. Always confirm against the recorded deeds and plats.

5. Build the adjoiner table

Run BCSADJ to place a table of adjoining owners with deed and plat references in the drawing. A CSV is saved beside the drawing too.

6. Refresh when records change

Run BCSREF to refresh every BIGCS label from county GIS by its stored PIN. It updates the text and date stamp and saves a CSV of changes.

7. Letters and door hangers (add-on)

With the Letters & Door Hangers add-on, BCSLTR opens BIGCS Letters in a separate window using the drawing's adjoiner CSV. Pick owners and a letter type (offer, mailer, notice of upcoming work) and produce letters outside of CAD.
