Privacy Policy

Draft, pending attorney review. Not legal advice.

Public-record information

Owner names, mailing and site addresses and similar details shown in BIGCS are public records published by government agencies. BIGCS shows them as the source provides them.

What we do not do

BIGCS does not sell personal data, and does not compile, store or market profiles of individuals. Data is retrieved live and placed in your drawing at your request.

Your responsibilities

  • Follow all applicable privacy, anti-harassment, anti-stalking and data-protection laws.
  • Do not use BIGCS data to harass, stalk, intimidate or harm anyone.
  • Do not use it for any purpose covered by the Fair Credit Reporting Act (15 U.S.C. § 1681 et seq.), such as decisions on credit, insurance, employment or tenant screening. BIGCS is not a consumer reporting agency.
  • Some jurisdictions let certain people shield their information. You are responsible for following those rules.

International data

Data for areas outside the U.S. has no owner names and is limited to what the government source publishes for free.

Corrections

If you believe information is inaccurate or should be suppressed, contact the publishing government agency. BIGCS cannot change source records.

This website

This site has no accounts or tracking cookies. If you email us, we use your message only to reply. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.
