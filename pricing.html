Questions

Licensing, cancellation, and where BIGCS runs.

What does per seat mean?

A seat is one user on one computer. Each plan is per seat. A two-person office that wants Whole State needs two seats.

Is the license key tied to one computer?

Yes. Each license key is tied to one computer. Your key is emailed from support@bigcsoftware.com.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel anytime by emailing support@bigcsoftware.com. Cancellation stops the next renewal. See the refund policy.

Which CAD programs does BIGCS work with?

Runs in Carlson, IntelliCAD and full AutoCAD. NC, SC, GA, VA and TN counties today; the coverage page shows what each county has. Data comes as-is from each county's public GIS. BIGCS never adjusts or datum-shifts it.

Can BIGCS draw building footprints?

Yes, in beta, for North Carolina only (not SC, GA or TX yet). Use BCSBLDG to pick a window or a parcel, or the Draw Building Footprints button in the Map Layers window (BCSMAP) for a whole county, up to 20,000 buildings. Footprints come from the NC Emergency Management statewide building footprints on NC OneMap and are drawn as closed polylines on layer BIGCS-BLDG in your drawing's coordinate system, with no datum shift. They have no labels, area or height. You can change the layer, color and linetype in BCSST. Included in every plan.

Are letters and door hangers included?

Letters & Door Hangers is an optional add-on for any plan: $9.99/mo or $99.90/yr per seat. Seats with the add-on can make letters, labels, and door hangers. Everything else works without it.
