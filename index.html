County GIS parcels, straight into your CAD drawing.

Features

What BIGCS does

Public records into real CAD entities: linework, text and properties you can work with.

PCL

Parcel boundaries + owner labels

Pick a point inside a parcel and BIGCS draws the boundary with owner, deed book/page, plat book/page and PIN text at true coordinates, straight from county GIS.

BCS

Aa

Custom label styles

Set line order, SHX or TrueType fonts, text height and justification (left, center, right and more), saved as reusable styles.

BCSST

T

Text-only insert

Place just the owner and record labels without the parcel linework, as a setting or its own command.

BCSTXT

BND

State & county boundaries

Draw state and county lines on demand. BIGCS remembers your last state and county, and the county list is type-to-search.

BCSBND

BLD

Building footprints (Beta)

Draw building outlines as closed polylines in your drawing's coordinate system, with no datum shift. Pick a window or a parcel (50 ft buffer by default), or a whole county with confirmation, up to 20,000 buildings. Source: NC Emergency Management statewide building footprints (NC OneMap), about 5.4 million buildings in all 100 NC counties. NC only for now. Outlines only: no labels, area or height. Included in every plan.

BCSBLDG

RD

Road centerlines

Pick an area to bring in road centerlines on their own layer, with the road name stored in the object properties, not as clutter text.

△

Survey monuments

Plot published control monuments in a picked area, with links back to the official datasheet.

FZ

FEMA flood zones

Bring in FEMA flood zone areas with a link to the flood map in each object's properties.

ADJ

Adjoiner table

Build a table of adjoining owners with deed and plat references, useful for surveyors, engineers, realtors and developers.

BCSADJ

⌕

Find by address or PIN

Jump to a parcel by site address or parcel number, alongside point-pick and area selection.

↻

Refresh

Re-pull a parcel from the county source to update labels when records change.

BCSREF

SRC

Source & date stamp

Each object records which GIS source it came from and when it was pulled.

✉

BIGCS Letters add-on

Pick owners and a letter type (offer, mailer, notice of upcoming work) and produce letters outside of CAD.

BCSLTR

BCS commands, with the original G2C and GIS2CAD names still working.
