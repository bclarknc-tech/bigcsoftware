What you get

Six outcomes for the drawing. Command names live on the command list.

PCL

Parcels and labels

Pick a point inside a parcel. BIGCS draws the boundary with owner, deed book/page, plat book/page and PIN text at true coordinates, straight from county GIS.

Aa

Label styles

Set line order, SHX or TrueType fonts, text height and justification, saved as reusable styles so the labels match your CAD standards.

ADJ

Adjoiner table

A table of adjoining owners with deed and plat references, placed in the drawing, with a CSV saved beside it.

MAP

Map layers

State and county lines, road centerlines, survey monuments and FEMA flood zones, on their own layers, for a county or a picked area.

BLD

Building footprints Beta

North Carolina only. Building outlines as closed polylines in the drawing's coordinate system, from NC Emergency Management statewide footprints on NC OneMap. Outlines only. Included in every plan.

LTR

Letters add-on

Pick owners and a letter type (offer, mailer, notice of upcoming work) and produce letters outside of CAD. Optional add-on for any plan.

How it works

Three steps. The labels land in the drawing you already have open.

  1. 1

    Pick a parcel

    Click inside the parcel, or find it by address or PIN.

  2. 2

    Pull GIS

    BIGCS reads the county parcel and the owner, deed and plat records.

  3. 3

    Labels at true coordinates

    The boundary and the record text are drawn in your coordinate system, in your label style.
