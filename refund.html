This policy covers paid subscriptions to BIGCS software, sold by Big C's Products, LLC, doing business as BIGCS Software, a North Carolina limited liability company. It works together with the Terms of Service.

Billing and cancellation

Subscriptions are billed in advance, per seat, monthly or yearly. Yearly plans are billed at the price of 10 months. Subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each billing period (monthly or yearly) until cancelled.

You may cancel at any time by emailing support@bigcsoftware.com. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period. Your license remains active through the end of the period you have paid for, after which access ends.

Refunds

Fees paid are non-refundable. Any refund is issued solely at the discretion of BIGCS Software. Questions regarding a charge may be directed to support@bigcsoftware.com.

License keys

Your license key and download link are emailed from sales@bigcsoftware.com. For help, email support@bigcsoftware.com. Each license key is tied to one computer. One seat is one user on one computer.
