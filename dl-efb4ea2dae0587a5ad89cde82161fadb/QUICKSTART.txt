BIGCS 1.1 - Quick Start
Parcel owner labels, adjoiners and deed info from county GIS, right in your CAD drawing.
States: North Carolina, South Carolina, Georgia, Virginia and Tennessee (other states on request).
Works in Carlson (IntelliCAD) and full AutoCAD. AutoCAD LT is not supported.

1. BIGCS loads automatically in every drawing
   - Carlson / IntelliCAD: the installer adds a line to icaddoc.lsp in your Carlson support folder.
   - AutoCAD: the installer adds a line to acaddoc.lsp in your AutoCAD user Support folder
     (%APPDATA%\Autodesk\AutoCAD <year>\R<ver>\enu\Support).
   - Close and reopen your CAD program after installing. The command line says "BIGCS loaded".
   - AutoCAD may show "Security - Unsigned Executable File" for BIGCS.lsp. Choose "Always load".
   - The BIGCS toolbar and menu load the first time. If the toolbar is hidden, right-click any toolbar and check BIGCS.
   - If BIGCS doesn't load (for example you installed your CAD program after BIGCS): type APPLOAD and
     load BIGCS.lsp from the install folder (default C:\Users\<you>\AppData\Local\Programs\BIGCS\BIGCS.lsp),
     or simply run the BIGCS installer again. Don't also add BIGCS.lsp to the Startup Suite.

2. Activate your license
   - Your Computer ID: type BCSLIC and press Enter (Status is the default). It shows "BIGCS computer ID: XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX".
   - Type BCSLIC, choose Enter key, and paste the key from your email (it starts with BCS- or BIGCS1.).
     You enter your key once. Each seat works on one computer.
   - Subscription keys (BCS-) renew by themselves: while your subscription is paid, BIGCS keeps working.
     Nothing to re-enter, ever. BIGCS re-checks online about once a day in the background.
   - If a payment fails, BIGCS keeps working for 10 days and shows how many days are left. Update your card,
     and BIGCS carries on by itself. After 10 days without payment the license goes inactive until you pay.
   - If you cancel, BIGCS keeps working until the end of the time you've paid for, then stops.
   - Your counties: BCSLIC > Status. To change counties or plans, email sales@bigcsoftware.com.
   - Trial keys (BCS-) cover one county for 40 uses or 14 days, whichever comes first. BCSLIC > Status shows the uses left
     and the end date. Each parcel pull, adjoiner run, radius or window pull, refresh or map-layer pull uses 1, and each one
     needs an internet connection. A pull that finds no parcel, fails or is cancelled does not use one. Batch/CSV pulls and
     Letters & Door Hangers are not part of the trial. To keep using BIGCS, email sales@bigcsoftware.com.
   - Keys from your welcome email that start with BIGCS1. (made for one Computer ID) show an end date;
     for those we email a renewal key after each payment.
   - BIGCS sets the drawing coordinate system to your state's State Plane (NAD83(2011), feet) when you activate:
       NC 6543   SC 6570 (intl ft)   TN 6576   GA East 6445 / West 6447   VA North 6593 / South 6595
     To use another one, type BCSCRS (or pick it in BCSBND). Each drawing keeps its own coordinate system;
     parcels and map layers from another state are converted into it, so they line up.
   - Offline: BIGCS works up to 14 days without internet (never past your paid time). It needs the internet to pull county data.
   - Updates: BIGCS checks bigcsoftware.com for county and style updates about once a day and installs them for you.

3. Your first parcel
   - Type BCS, choose Single, and click inside a parcel in a drawing that is in State Plane.
     BIGCS draws the parcel and places the owner, deed book/page and PID label.
   - The first time, a "BIGCS - Disclaimer" window opens. Read it and click I Agree. It does not ask again.
   - Not in State Plane yet? Type BCSBND, draw your county boundary, and zoom to your area.
   - Commands:
     BCS      Get Parcels (single, adjoiners, radius, window)
     BCSTXT   Labels only
     BCSADJ   Adjoiner table + CSV
     BCSREF   Refresh labels
     BCSLTR   Letters and door hangers (Letters add-on)
     BCSMAP   Map Layers: borders, monuments, roads, flood zones, easements, buildings (also BCSBND)
     BCSBLDG  Building footprints (NC, SC, GA, VA, TN) in a window or around a parcel
     BCSLINK  Open an object's deed / datasheet link
     BCSCRS   This drawing's coordinate system (show / change)
     BCSROT   Turn existing labels to a line you pick
     BCSST    Label style editor
     BCSLIC   License (Status shows your Computer ID, Enter key, Deactivate)

4. Moving to a new computer
   - Your key works only on the computer (and Windows user) it was made for. Another computer refuses it.
   - On the old computer: BCSLIC > Deactivate. Then install BIGCS on the new computer and enter the same BCS- key.
     (2 moves in any 30 days per seat. Old computer not available? Email support@bigcsoftware.com.)
   - BIGCS1. keys: email support@bigcsoftware.com the new Computer ID (BCSLIC > Status). We send a key for it.

5. Uninstall
   - Windows Settings > Apps > BIGCS > Uninstall. It removes the BIGCS lines from icaddoc.lsp / acaddoc.lsp
     and leaves your own lines in those files alone. In CAD, type MENUUNLOAD and pick BIGCS.

Building footprints available in NC, SC, GA, VA and TN (BCSBLDG, or Map Layers > Draw Building Footprints).
Footprint data: NC Emergency Management (NC); USA Structures, ORNL/FEMA, CC BY 4.0 (SC, GA, VA, TN).

BIGCS is not a survey. County GIS data can be wrong or out of date. Read DISCLAIMER.txt and EULA.txt in the install folder.
